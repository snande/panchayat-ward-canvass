# Rajasthan Panchayati Raj general election 2026: the SEC's orders

Desk research, 2026-10-06, by a Claude research agent reading the SEC's own
PDFs (scanned Hindi) plus press for the latest revision; reviewed by the
facilitator. [confirmed] = SEC document or press reporting an SEC order;
[tentative] = inferred. Primary source: press note and Annexures A/B, order
9836 of 03.10.2026, https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/9836.pdf.

## 1. Schedule and phases

Original schedule (order 9836, Annexure A) [confirmed]:
- ZP members: notification 08.10, nominations 08 to 13.10 (11:00 to 15:00), scrutiny 14.10, withdrawal 15.10.
- PS members, phases 1 to 4: notification 08.10 / 13.10 / 19.10 / 31.10; last nomination 13.10 / 16.10 / 23.10 / 04.11; scrutiny 14.10 / 17.10 / 24.10 / 05.11; withdrawal 15.10 / 19.10 / 26.10 / 06.11.
- ZP and PS polling (EVM): 23.10 / 29.10 / 04.11 / 14.11, 07:00 to 18:00.
- Panch and sarpanch: nomination day 24.10 / 30.10 / 05.11 / 15.11 (file 08:00 to 11:00, scrutiny 11:30, withdrawal till 14:00, symbols immediately after); polling the next day 25.10 / 31.10 / 06.11 / 16.11, 07:00 to 18:00; counting the same day at GP HQ; up-sarpanch the next day.
- ZP and PS counting, all phases: 20.11.2026 from 08:00 at district HQ. Zila Pramukh 25.11, Up-Zila Pramukh 26.11, Pradhan 27.11, Up-Pradhan 28.11.

Nomination revision (order 9867, 03.10.2026) [confirmed] https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/9867.pdf: ZP member nominations follow the phase of the district's first PS phase. 17 ZPs in phase 1 (Ajmer, Alwar, Barmer, Bhilwara, Bikaner, Dungarpur, Jaipur, Jaisalmer, Jhalawar, Jodhpur, Phalodi, Kota, Nagaur, Pali, Rajsamand, Sirohi, Udaipur); 7 in phase 2 (Banswara, Dausa, Kotputli-Behror, Jalore, Jhunjhunu, Karauli, Sikar); 16 in phase 3 (Beawar, Khairthal-Tijara, Baran, Balotra, Bharatpur, Deeg, Bundi, Chittorgarh, Churu, Dholpur, Hanumangarh, Didwana-Kuchaman, Pratapgarh, Sawai Madhopur, Sri Ganganagar, Tonk); phase 4 Salumbar only. Pramukh and Pradhan polls for Baran, Sri Ganganagar, Karauli and Kota ZPs are deferred (terms end Dec 2026).

## 2. Ballot box vs EVM, and counting [confirmed]

- Orders 1711 (13.02.2026) and 9809 (02.10.2026): sarpanch and ward panch by ballot box; all ZP and PS wards by EVM. https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/9809.pdf
- Ballot: sarpanch white, panch pink; symbols pre-printed in strips of ten, candidate names hand-written at GP level after nomination; NOTA sticker after the last candidate.
- Counting (order 1323, 03.02.2026, https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/13230001.pdf): immediately after close of poll at GP HQ under the RO; result declared the same day; the RO may recount on request where the margin is very small. ZP and PS: 20.11 at district HQ from 08:00.

## 3. Polling hours [confirmed]

07:00 to 18:00 for all tiers; voters in the compound at 18:00 are queued and voted (press note p.4).

## 4. Silence period and poll-day rules

- SEC MCC booklet 2026 (https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/Upload/MCC_Acharan_sahita_BOOK_FINAL_2026.pdf) [confirmed]: no public meetings in the 48 hours ending at close of poll; no canvassing within 100 m of a polling station; no candidate camp within 200 m; camps plain, no posters, flags or symbols, no food, no crowds; voter slips plain white with no symbol, candidate or party; no liquor on poll day and 48 hours before.
- Order 5052 (23.12.2025): loudspeakers banned 48 hours before close of poll and 22:00 to 06:00; no rally or convoy of more than 3 vehicles in the last 48 hours; vehicle caps ZP 3, PS 2, sarpanch 1 [confirmed].
- Order 972 (24.01.2026, https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/972.pdf): on poll day no campaigning within 100 m of the polling building; RPA s.130 applies (fine up to Rs 250) [confirmed].
- Mobile phones (order 685, 20.01.2026, https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/cm/upload/68520260120.pdf): no one may carry or use a mobile inside a booth or within 100 m; police on duty confiscate and return on exit; also banned at counting centres for candidates and agents; election staff and observers exempt but on silent [confirmed]. No carve-out for polling agents.
- A rule on SMS, social media or digital campaigning in the silence window: not found in any SEC order or the MCC booklet.

## 5. Expenditure ceiling [confirmed]

ZP member Rs 3,00,000; PS member Rs 1,50,000; sarpanch Rs 1,00,000 (order 5052 and press note p.5). Panch: no ceiling prescribed. Account in Form "ka" within 15 days of the declaration of result to the DEO (Panchayat), displayed 3 days on the notice board, objections 7 days. Violation: fine up to Rs 2,000 and disqualification up to 6 years (s.22A).

## 6. Turnout and results online [tentative]

The SEC site has public report pages: ZP members ("Polling percentage", "Polling booth report", winners, candidate-wise votes) at https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/ZP_Election_Reports.aspx; GP ("Progress of Poll", winner sarpanch, winning panch) at https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/GP_Election_Reports.aspx; PS at PS_Election_Reports.aspx; results hub https://sec.rajasthan.gov.in/Announcement.aspx (ULB 2026 results are live there). Pages are empty until data is fed. Intra-day two-hourly booth-wise turnout: not found in any order; 2026 ULB turnout appeared in press as "by 3 pm 72.66%" only.

## 7. Apps, voter slips, candidate booths

- e-suchi EVM tracking and randomisation software for DEOs; NIC randomisation of staff; a 24x7 control room 0141-2227407 (press note) [confirmed]. No voter-facing app found.
- Candidate booths (order 972): not within 100 m of the polling entrance, not at religious sites, schools or hospitals; one table, two chairs, umbrella or 10x10 ft tent; written RO permission; used only to hand out slips bearing name, roll serial, ward and booth, with no party or candidate name or symbol [confirmed].
- Voter Assistance Booth with BLO staff at every polling building (order 9326, 24.09.2026) [confirmed].
- Victory processions banned under BNSS s.163 (order 9961) [confirmed].

## What this changes for the plan

- The first panch and sarpanch poll is now 24 Oct, eighteen days from this
  note; Jaipur district spans all four phases, so a friend's phase depends
  on their panchayat samiti (Annexure B).
- Phones are banned within 100 m of the booth with no carve-out for agents,
  and candidate camps sit beyond 200 m; the tally design in decision 0005
  is consistent with both.
- Sarpanch and panch counts happen at GP HQ the same evening; a results
  screen for those tiers is one number per candidate entered once, so it
  stays in scope only if it costs nothing (decision 0007).
- Sarpanch ceiling is Rs 1,00,000 and the account is due 15 days after the
  result; a free MVP has no expenditure consequence, a paid one must appear
  in Form "ka".
