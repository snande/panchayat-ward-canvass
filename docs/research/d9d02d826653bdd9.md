# panchayat-ward-canvass v2: build brief

Source: the initiative's software-v2 dossier (draft 2, 2026-10-09), the sections that bear on the build. Pricing, the client's verbatim answers and intake process notes are omitted because this repository is public.

## Target Mandate

### objective

A professional, installable Hindi campaign app (PWA, own domain) that any
ward panch or sarpanch candidate in Rajasthan can run with their team in
the remaining 2026 polls: it loads their constituency's roll from the
State Election Commission, supports the door-to-door canvass with
supporter tags, household visits, worker assignment and consented phone
numbers, coordinates calls, voter slips and the candidate's appeal from
workers' own phones, runs polling day from a per-booth list of supporters
not yet voted, records the count, and gives the candidate one view of
the campaign. Each candidate's data is private to their team.

### done_conditions

1. **Any panchayat.** A candidate picks seat type (ward panch or
   sarpanch) and their panchayat and ward from a catalogue covering every
   rural ward in Rajasthan; a sarpanch gets every ward of the panchayat.
   The app downloads and decodes each roll with no upload. Struck-off
   entries are shown as struck off, not dropped, and supplementary
   additions and deletions appear when the SEC publishes them (deletions
   hidden by default, with a toggle). Every screen names the loaded
   panchayat and ward. Checked on five panchayats in five districts.
2. **Search everything.** Offline, a worker finds a voter by part of the
   name, relative's name, house number, serial or EPIC, typed in Hindi or
   in English letters ("ramesh" finds रमेश), across all the
   constituency's wards. Results show serial, name, relative, age, gender
   and house, with the match highlighted, and can be narrowed by ward or
   booth, gender, age range, tag, visit status, "has number" and "not
   called", and sorted by relevance, serial, name or age. Typing "3/145"
   opens ward 3, serial 145.
3. **Households.** Voters at one house number appear as one household
   card showing each member's tag, number and visit status; one tap
   opens any member.
4. **One voter card.** Everywhere it opens, the same card shows the full
   roll line (name, relation and relative, age, gender, house, EPIC,
   serial, ward, booth name and address) and the team's tag, notes, phone
   number with who saved it and when, and call and visit history, with a
   button to copy or share the voter's details.
5. **Tags and visits.** A worker tags a voter as supporter, leaning,
   undecided, opposed, away or not found, adds a note, and marks the
   household visited, not at home or revisit. Every change carries the
   worker's name and time, reaches teammates within a minute when online,
   and keeps its history.
6. **Assignment.** A coordinator assigns roll pages or households to
   workers (the panna pramukh way), and each worker sees "my households"
   with progress.
7. **Calls.** A coordinator builds a call list by filter (for example
   supporters with a number not called in three days) and assigns it; a
   worker calls from their own phone and records the outcome (answered,
   no answer, wrong number, call back, support confirmed); follow-ups
   come back on the list.
8. **Slips and appeal.** A worker sends a voter a plain voter slip (the
   voter's name, serial, ward, booth name and address, poll date and
   hours; no candidate, party or symbol, as the SEC's code requires) as
   an image through their own WhatsApp, one voter at a time, or prints a
   booth's slips. Separately, the candidate's appeal card (name, photo,
   symbol, slogan) can be shared the same way, except in the 48 hours
   before the close of poll, when the app hides it.
9. **Polling day.** On the candidate's poll date the app opens in
   polling-day mode: for each booth, the list of supporters not yet seen
   voting, ordered by house; marking takes one tap and can be undone for
   five minutes; the counts reach the team within a minute online, or by
   a prefilled SMS with no data. A banner reminds workers that phones are
   barred within 100 m of the booth. Before polling day the "voted"
   action is not offered.
10. **Booth board.** The coordinator sees, per booth, supporters, seen
    voting, remaining, time of the last update and the official turnout
    once entered, and can send a worker the names to fetch.
11. **Counting.** At the count that evening, the counting agent enters
    votes per candidate (per table where there is more than one); the app
    totals them and shows the margin.
12. **Candidate's view.** One screen shows households visited, numbers
    captured, tags by booth, calls and outcomes, each worker's activity,
    and roll statistics (totals reconciled with the roll's printed
    summary, gender, age bands, first-time voters, voters over 80), with
    printable lists per booth and per worker. The operator sees the same
    counts per team, never a voter, tag or number, to measure the field
    test.
13. **Team and setup.** A candidate starts a team with an activation code
    the operator issues, sets their branding (name, photo, symbol,
    colours, slogan, WhatsApp Channel link, support number), invites
    workers by link or QR code, gives each a role (coordinator or
    worker) and can remove one; a removed worker's phone loses the team's
    data the next time it opens.
14. **Onboarding and updates.** A new worker installs the app from a
    WhatsApp link with a one-screen Hindi install guide; the app asks for
    persistent storage on first run; a practice mode with a sample ward
    lets them try every action without touching real data; an installed
    app says when a new version is ready and updates in one tap.
15. **Reliability.** Twenty phones on two mobile carriers marking 3,000
    voters through a simulated polling day, with airplane-mode stretches
    and SMS fallback, end with a team count equal to the distinct voters
    marked: nothing lost, nothing counted twice. A phone offline for a
    day loses nothing.
16. **Privacy and retention.** No other candidate and not the operator
    can read a team's data. The candidate can delete all of it at any
    time. After the result the app asks the candidate to keep or delete
    it; kept data is held while their licence or test term runs and is
    deleted, after a warning, when it ends.

### context

For ward panch and sarpanch candidates in Rajasthan's panchayat elections
and the relatives and workers who run their campaigns: small teams on 2
to 4 GB Android phones, in villages with patchy signal, canvassing door
to door before a nomination that falls the day before the poll. The
first users are the client's friends in the remaining 2026 phases, free;
the same app is sold per candidate per election from 2027. Version 1 was
a field test for one panchayat (Badli); this one has to look and work
like a product a candidate pays for and recommends.

### non_goals

- Automated or bulk calling, SMS or WhatsApp of any kind; the WhatsApp
  Business Platform.
- Native Android or iOS apps; a Play Store listing.
- Panchayat samiti and zila parishad members (a later phase).
- Licence sales, payment and a seller's console (a later phase; this
  Mandate uses operator-issued activation codes).
- Urban (municipal) rolls, which are scanned and need OCR, until an
  experiment shows they decode reliably.
- States other than Rajasthan (the roll code stays behind an adapter so
  a state can be added later).
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads (Meta's authorisation has not been started).
- Any voter data not on the roll other than consented phone numbers,
  tags and notes the team enters: no purchased lists, no social-media
  data, no caste, religion or community fields or inference.
- Vote prediction or scoring of voters.

### constraints

- Installed PWA on Android Chrome, on the product's own domain; opens on
  a 2 GB phone; works fully offline after the roll is loaded.
- Rolls come from the SEC's public PDFs by text-layer glyph matching; no
  OCR, no upload, no roll prepared by hand. The catalogue is generated
  from the SEC portal by a script that can be re-run.
- Consent is recorded before a phone number is saved. EPIC numbers,
  phone numbers, tags, notes and marks are encrypted on the phone and on
  the server, readable only by the candidate's team.
- The sync server keeps each candidate's records in a strongly
  consistent store (no lost writes when two phones sync at once) and
  does not depend on free-tier quotas.
- A team can only be started with an operator-issued activation code,
  and joining is rate-limited, so a team password cannot be guessed and
  a candidate's code cannot be taken by someone else.
- Calls, WhatsApp messages, slips and appeals go from a worker's own
  phone, one voter at a time. Slips follow the SEC's code: plain, with
  no candidate, party or symbol.
- The product is non-partisan: party names, colours and symbols appear
  only as a candidate's own branding, and every screen's footer gives
  the data source, says it is not an official SEC app, and that the
  printed roll prevails.
- Every setup step the operator must do (secrets, bindings, DNS, plan
  upgrades, activation codes) is filed as an operator issue, not left in
  README prose.

### quality_bar

- **Professional and consistent.** Every screen is built from DESIGN.md's
  components; one voter card; every surface has its loading, empty,
  error and success states, and every error says what to do and whom to
  call; a real icon and splash in the candidate's branding; light and
  dark themes. Fit: the operator and two candidates judge it "looks like
  a product I would pay for".
- **Fast.** Cold start under 3 s on a 2 GB phone. Search p95 under
  200 ms over a 10,000-voter roll, measured in CI in headless Chrome
  with 4x CPU throttling, and confirmed on a phone by the operator.
- **Never loses or double counts.** A randomised simulation of 20
  workers across sync, offline queues, SMS, retries and replays, run
  against the real server store locally (not an in-memory fake), passes
  100 of 100 runs in CI.
- **Readable outdoors.** Body text contrast at least 7:1 in the light
  theme, text at least 16 px with a larger-text setting, tap targets at
  least 48 px; zero serious accessibility violations in CI.
- **Natural Hindi.** All copy reviewed by a native Hindi speaker.

### client_responsibilities

- Hosting on a paid plan (Cloudflare Workers Paid or equal) and the
  domain; the secrets and bindings each operator issue asks for.
- Activation codes for each candidate team.
- A legal read of supporter tagging, the appeal card and data retention
  against the SEC's orders and the DPDP Act.
- Each candidate's branding: name, photo, symbol, colours, slogan,
  WhatsApp Channel link and support number.
- A native Hindi reviewer for the app's copy.
- Test phones: at least one 2 GB phone, and phones on Jio and Airtel.
- The friends' teams, their panchayats and polling phases, and a short
  interview after each phase.

### target_date

`2026-10-17` (one week before the first panch and sarpanch poll).

## Suggested build order

The CEO authors the phases; this order keeps polling day safe even if the
last phases slip:

(a) Stabilise: strongly consistent sync, paid hosting, activation codes
    and join limits, update prompt, one voter card.
(b) Roll and search: state catalogue, sarpanch's whole panchayat, booth
    details, struck-off and supplementary entries, full search, filters
    and sort, households.
(c) Canvass and team: tags, visits, attribution, assignment, notes,
    roles, invites, branding, onboarding and practice mode.
(d) Polling day and counting: polling-day mode, the not-yet-voted list,
    booth board, SMS fallback, counting entry.
(e) Calls, slips and appeal.
(f) Candidate's view, statistics, printing and data retention.

## Later (2027 paid product)

- Panchayat samiti and zila parishad members: constituencies spanning
  many panchayats, mapped from the roll covers; rolls up to about 40,000
  voters.
- Licences and the seller's console: issue and expire licences per
  candidate per election, branding, usage counts, never voter data.
- Payment collection (UPI) if the client wants it in the product.
- English interface toggle.
- Other states through the roll adapter; urban rolls after an OCR
  experiment; click-to-WhatsApp ads after experiment 0004.
